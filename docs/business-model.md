# Business Model

Status: Intended model; pricing, service scope, and payment arrangements remain proposals. See the [README](../README.md) for the canonical overview.

## Earn recurring revenue

**90-day free trial → affordable monthly subscription → recurring revenue → sustainable maintenance and support.**

The trial should be generous enough for a candidate to experience real value before being asked to pay. No final subscription price is set.

> If a school-board candidate cannot identify enough value after 90 days to willingly pay an affordable monthly subscription, we have not earned the subscription.

Revenue should eventually cover hosting, maintenance, development, security, customer support, and compensated workers. Volunteer contributions can help bootstrap the project; essential operations should not depend permanently on unpaid labor.

## Open source without artificial lock-in

Software inspectability and forkability should coexist with a paid managed service. The subscription would pay for dependable operation and support, not deliberate obstacles to leaving.

Design for useful exports, documented formats, and interoperable workflows. Cancellation, retention, deletion, and migration behavior need explicit design before launch. Do not imply those capabilities already exist.

## Questions to validate

- What can a candidate accomplish during the trial that existing tools do not already make easy?
- What price is affordable while covering realistic support and infrastructure costs?
- How should campaigns pause, close, or retain service between elections?
- How should variable email/SMS or other integration costs be handled transparently?
- What workload and revenue would support compensated workers rather than founder subsidy?
- What cooperative legal form, membership rights, and financial oversight fit the service?

Measure willingness to pay alongside operating cost and support burden. Free-trial adoption alone does not establish sustainability. Trial-to-paid consent and billing terms remain to be designed; the trial is not a justification for surprise charges.

## Future: funding schools through payment infrastructure

[Forever Funding](https://foreverfunding.help/) is contemplated as Political Co-op's merchant/payment processor, not as the product's central pitch or an established partnership. Through that processor, Political Co-op could nominate eligible schools or school-supporting nonprofit entities to receive a portion of merchant-processing economics.

The long-term policy concept is that **every subscribed school-board campaign should result in some funding benefiting the school/community it seeks to serve, regardless of whether the candidate wins or loses.** Recipient selection and funding must remain politically neutral and structurally separate from candidate outcomes.

This is an architectural/business concept, not a live arrangement or a guaranteed allocation. Processor capabilities and terms, recipient eligibility, allocation mechanics, disclosures, and applicable legal/compliance requirements need verification and appropriate review before implementation. No claim is made about legal permissibility or campaign-finance treatment. This proposal does not authorize routing candidate donations through that mechanism.
