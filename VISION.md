# Vision

[The README](README.md) is the canonical short explanation. This document expands the intended direction, not an approved implementation plan.

## A service that survives election cycles

Political Co-op is a proposed politically neutral infrastructure cooperative. Its first product is an affordable School Board Campaign OS. Start with a concrete local-campaign problem, learn from candidates, and retain useful capability across elections.

The working [early proof of concept](https://guywhoknowstheguy.github.io/school-board-campaign-os-demo/) makes the idea tangible. It does not select the production architecture. Its source is not copied into this repository.

## What should persist

**Build once. Steward forever.** Software will change; accumulated operational capability should not disappear with a campaign, a vendor, or a founder.

Preserve reusable workflows, maintained tools, security practices, documented decisions, and lessons from postmortems. Do not confuse institutional memory with indefinite retention of personal data or sharing campaign records. Retention, consent, deletion, and lawful reuse need explicit design.

A successful service should earn voluntary subscriptions after a 90-day free trial and use recurring revenue to support reliable operations and paid workers. Affordability must coexist with the real cost of maintenance and support.

## Independence and continuity

Candidates of every party and no party should receive equal terms. The initial bootstrap community's affiliations confer no right to steer the infrastructure toward a party's advantage. Political neutrality must survive changes in personnel and governance.

CIO-first formation means treating documentation, recovery, access governance, and institutional memory as foundational work. Technology authority is not automatically political or governance authority.

Design from inception for progressive youth operation and control over a minimum 20-year transition. Recruiting youth contributors early is possible, not a launch dependency. The immediate engineering task is to remove founder dependency and make knowledge transferable.

## What this proposal does not settle

The MVP, technology stack, cooperative legal form, membership rights, governance mechanics, pricing, and succession milestones are open. This is not a party organization, candidate endorsement program, or finished software service.

The next useful step is evidence: identify a small set of candidate problems worth solving, compare existing tools, and test whether a sustainable service can solve them. [RFC #1](rfcs/0001-minimum-viable-school-board-campaign-os.md) starts that discussion.
